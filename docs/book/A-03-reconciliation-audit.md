# A-03. Reconciliation audit

Book **v1.0** · 2026-11-14 · First tracked manuscript release.

## Meaning and evidence boundary

This is the completed author-side reconciliation audit of the released design. It consolidates the whole-book reconciliation with a fresh substantive owner check for every one of the 83 stable requirements. Each row names the Part I and Part II sections that carry its mechanism or explicit scope boundary; a requirement-ID occurrence alone did not establish coverage. The nine completion criteria are those in II-16 §32 / Figure F-227 and the governing assignment.

**PASS-D** means the document design and trace are covered at the declared phase, after the corrections listed here. It does not mean the implementation exists or a native compatibility test passed. Later scope remains later; excluded execution/server/format/dialect requirements are checked as boundaries. Four owner/coverage inconsistencies found in this audit were corrected before release (RC-11–14); earlier reconciliation corrections RC-01–10 are recorded separately so that “what was corrected” is inspectable rather than implied.

All actual ACE/Access/CalcDb/COM/managed/browser/crash/repair/performance acceptance remains planned and unexecuted. The user-reported initial oracle is ACEDAO.DLL 16.0.20527.20016; loaded identities still require capture. Figures are emitted, not render-verified. Reading the source ledger is distinct from rereading every external source; exact prior inspection extents remain in A-01. This author audit is input to, not a substitute for, the independent A-04 Claude review.
