# A-03. Reconciliation audit

Book **v1.1** · 2026-11-16 · [Release record](00-book-release.md).

## Meaning and evidence boundary

This is the author reconciliation of the v1.1 manuscript. It checks the current scope map against its owning chapters, reassesses the independent v1.0 review's named gaps, records the outcome of the closed revision list and recounts the published inventories. The exact released Markdown bytes and reading order are identified by `book-manifest.v1.1.json`. The scope is [00-scope.md](00-scope.md), §§S1–S7, governed by final PRD-01–09 and the accepted PRW-01–58 list, with PRW-37 dropped. [B-SCOPE-11, U-PRD-01-09, U-PRW-01-58]

The supplied A-04 remains an independent review of v1.0. Its findings are revision inputs; they are neither observations of ACE nor an independent review of v1.1. Its §5.4 challenged the old uniform PASS-D label for 23 requirements. Each of those rows has an explicit outcome below. RC-01–14 retain the v1.0 correction history; subsequent entries record the v1.1 changes and this reconciliation's corrections. [L-REVIEW-F52-V10]

An outcome in this audit concerns the stated design, scope or document obligation. It does not mean that CalcDb exists or that a native compatibility test passed. All ACE/Access/CalcDb, COM/Automation, VBA/CalcVBA, managed, browser, crash, Compact & Repair and performance experiments remain specified and unexecuted. The supplied ACEDAO.DLL 16.0.20527.20016 report and Office C2R 16.0.20527.20150 registration report remain distinct; actual loaded identities must be captured. [U-OWNER-ACE, U-PRD-01-09]

PRD-07 supplies the crash-parity goal and the record/rebuild/independent-opener direction. The attainable classification and precise comparison rule remain implementation work. PRD-08 supplies the per-write damage/check direction and illustrative cases; the closed list and precise completed-write pass condition also remain implementation work. The requirement table and completion criteria do not turn those directions into settled acceptance rules. Date/Time Extended's full public projection and shared operations remain a named design gap. The ADO asynchronous-option experiment retains its possible conflict with the blocking-call contract.

Source inspection keeps the exact extents in A-01. This reconciliation does not claim a fresh full read of every external source, recovered original research-note attachments, or executed examples from absent checker scripts. Figures are rendered by the PDF build; the rendering report records actual inspection coverage separately. No PDF rendering or visual-inspection result is inferred from this release.

**Design supplied** means that the cited chapters provide a preferred mechanism, its limits and the deciding evidence or acceptance work. **Later design supplied** preserves the stated later phase. **Boundary recorded** and **constraint retained** describe an exclusion or conditional obligation, not a successful implementation. **Retained gap** names missing integration/design closure. **Direction supplied; acceptance open** applies where PRD-07/08 deliberately leave precise classification, method or pass conditions to implementation. These outcomes must not be collapsed into a uniform pass label.

The requirement text and phase below are copied verbatim from I-16’s current scope map. The references identify substantive owner sections, not merely ID occurrences. The meanings of *qualified* and *established* remain those in [book-record, “Terminology”](book-record.md#terminology). The nine completion obligations are retained in II-16 §32/F-227 from the supplied assignment. [A]
